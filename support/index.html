Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Write to lasttin.app@gmail.com. We usually answer within a few days.

Manage or cancel your subscription

Apple handles the subscription. You can change or cancel it at any time:

  • in the app: Settings → Manage subscription, or
  • on your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Last Tin, or
  • directly at apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.

Cancel at least 24 hours before your free days end or the current month ends, and you won’t be charged again. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

Refunds

Payments go to Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple too. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Restore your purchase

On a new iPhone or after reinstalling the app, open Last Tin and go through the setup steps. On the subscription page that follows, tap Restore at the bottom, below the subscription terms. If your access has ended, the same page appears when you open the app. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe.

Your data

Everything you log stays on your iPhone. There is no account and no sync, so we cannot see or restore your entries. If you delete the app, your entries are deleted with it. On a new iPhone they come along only if you set it up from a backup of your old iPhone.

Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Widgets and Control Center

To log a pouch without opening the app, add the Last Tin widget to your Home Screen or Lock Screen, or add the Last Tin button to Control Center: touch and hold an empty area, tap Edit, then add a widget or a control and choose Last Tin.

No reminder?

The reminder comes at most once a day, and only on days when you haven’t noted anything yet. If it never arrives, check that it is on in the app’s settings and that notifications for Last Tin are allowed in the iOS Settings under Notifications.
