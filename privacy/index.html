Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

In short:

  • Everything you log in Last Tin stays on your iPhone. There is no account, no server of ours and no cloud sync.
  • Only if you agree, the app sends a few usage events under a pseudonymous identifier to the analytics service TelemetryDeck, for example “the app was used today”. They never contain amounts, texts, reasons or prices, and you can withdraw your consent in the app at any time.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Apple.

Who is responsible

Last Tin is made by Adnan Cehaja, who is the controller responsible for your data (“we” on this page). For any question about your data, write to lasttin.app@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Last Tin stores the following only on your iPhone:

  • your entries (each pouch with its time and day), cravings with their outcome and confirmed zero days,
  • your daily limit, the pouches a day before you started, the price per can and the pouches per can,
  • your reasons, in your own words,
  • your reminder settings.

The app keeps this data in a storage area on your iPhone that only Last Tin and its widgets can use. We have no access to it, and nothing of it is sent to us or anyone else. There is no account and no sync with iCloud or other devices.

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud Backup or on a computer, iOS includes this data in the backup, as it does for other apps. That backup is handled by Apple, not by us. If you delete the app, the data is deleted with it, and we cannot restore it.

Widgets and Control Center

The widgets on the Home Screen and Lock Screen and the Control Center button read and write the same data on your iPhone as the app. They send nothing except the usage events described below.

Reminders

The daily reminder is a local notification that the app schedules on your iPhone. No server is involved. Its text does not mention nicotine, pouches or your reasons. You can turn it off in the app or in the iOS Settings at any time.

Subscription

You buy and manage the subscription through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes the payment under its own privacy policy; we do not receive your name, your Apple Account or your payment details. On your iPhone, the app asks the App Store whether your free trial or subscription is active, when it started and when it ends. It uses this to unlock the app and to count the days since your trial started (see below).

Through App Store Connect, Apple gives us aggregated figures such as downloads, sales, refunds and renewals. If you have chosen in the iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple also gives us anonymous app usage statistics.

Usage events

To know whether Last Tin is useful, for example whether people who started the free trial still use it a week later, the app sends a few usage events to TelemetryDeck under a pseudonymous identifier (see below), but only if you have agreed (see Your consent). We only look at aggregated numbers.

Undoing an entry sends nothing. The events never contain amounts, limits, savings, prices, your reasons or any other text.

Events first wait in a small outbox on your iPhone until they have been handed to TelemetryDeck. This way none is lost when you are offline, though one may occasionally arrive twice.

What TelemetryDeck adds

TelemetryDeck’s software in the app adds the following to each event:

  • A pseudonymous identifier. It is derived from the “identifier for vendor” that iOS assigns to the apps of one developer on one device. The app turns it into a SHA-256 hash before sending, and TelemetryDeck hashes it again on its servers. It contains neither your name nor your Apple Account, but it stays the same for all events from your iPhone. It only changes when all apps from our developer account have been removed from the iPhone. The app does not use the advertising identifier and does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • The time of sending. Because events can wait in the outbox, this may be later than the use itself.
  • Technical details: app version and build, device model, processor architecture, platform, operating system and its version, app language, preferred language, region and locale, time zone, screen size, orientation, light or dark mode, text direction, accessibility settings (such as larger text, bold text or reduced motion), calendar details of the time of sending (such as weekday and hour), whether the app runs from the App Store, from TestFlight or as a development build, for events from a widget or Control Center which kind of extension sent it, the version of TelemetryDeck’s software, a random session number and whether the event is a test.

TelemetryDeck’s software would also add session statistics to each event: the date of the first session, the number of days and sessions with use and the length of sessions. Last Tin switches these off and sends these fields empty. The software keeps its own list of the days on which it was active in the app’s storage on your iPhone; that list is not sent.

When sending, your iPhone necessarily connects to TelemetryDeck’s servers with its IP address; according to TelemetryDeck, IP addresses are not stored.

TelemetryDeck is operated by TelemetryDeck GmbH in Augsburg, Germany, and processes the events on servers in the European Union. TelemetryDeck currently has no fixed schedule for deleting stored events and expects to delete them after seven to ten years. More in TelemetryDeck’s privacy FAQ.

In its Data Processing Agreement, which is part of its terms of service, TelemetryDeck commits to technical and organizational security measures and to processing the events only in a form that does not allow it to identify you. TelemetryDeck thus protects your data at least as well as described in this policy.

Your consent

During setup, the app asks whether you want to share usage statistics. Only if you agree does it record and send usage events; if you don’t, it sends none. You can change your choice at any time: in the app, open Settings and turn Share usage statistics on or off. Once you turn it off, the app records and sends no more events, also not from widgets or Control Center, and it discards the events still waiting in the outbox. Events handed to TelemetryDeck in the seconds before may still be delivered. Withdrawing your consent does not affect the processing that took place before.

Email

If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to answer you and delete them when they are no longer needed. The mailbox is hosted by Google (Gmail).

Legal basis

Last Tin is operated from Germany, so the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies. We process the usage events only with your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Storing and reading the information on your iPhone that this requires, such as the identifier for vendor and the outbox, is based on the same consent (Section 25(1) of the German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act, TDDDG). You can withdraw your consent at any time with the switch described above. Emails are processed to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). The data you log in the app is not processed by us at all, because it never leaves your iPhone.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object to processing, the right to withdraw your consent at any time and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. To withdraw your consent to the usage events, turn off Share usage statistics. We cannot see which identifier belongs to you, so we usually cannot find your events in TelemetryDeck on our own. To delete everything you logged, delete the app. For all other requests, write to lasttin.app@gmail.com.

Age

Last Tin is intended for adults aged 18 and over.

Changes

If the app starts handling data differently, we will update this page and its date.
