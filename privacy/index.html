Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

In short:

  • Everything you log in Last Tin stays on your iPhone. There is no account, no server of ours and no cloud sync.
  • The app sends a few anonymous usage events to the analytics service TelemetryDeck, for example “an entry was made today”. They never contain amounts, texts, reasons or prices.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Apple.

Who is responsible

Last Tin is made by an independent developer. For any question about your data, write to lasttin.app@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Last Tin stores the following only on your iPhone:

  • your entries (each pouch with its time and day), cravings with their outcome and confirmed zero days,
  • your daily limit, the pouches a day before you started, the price per can and the pouches per can,
  • your reasons, in your own words,
  • your reminder settings.

The app keeps this data in a storage area on your iPhone that only Last Tin and its widgets can use. We have no access to it, and nothing of it is sent to us or anyone else. There is no account and no sync with iCloud or other devices.

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud Backup or on a computer, iOS includes this data in the backup, as it does for other apps. That backup is handled by Apple, not by us. If you delete the app, the data is deleted with it, and we cannot restore it.

Widgets and Control Center

The widgets on the Home Screen and Lock Screen and the Control Center button read and write the same data on your iPhone as the app. They send nothing except the anonymous usage events described below.

Reminders

The daily reminder is a local notification that the app schedules on your iPhone. No server is involved. Its text does not mention nicotine, pouches or your reasons. You can turn it off in the app or in the iOS Settings at any time.

Subscription

You buy and manage the subscription through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes the payment under its own privacy policy; we do not receive your name, your Apple Account or your payment details. On your iPhone, the app asks the App Store whether your free trial or subscription is active, when it started and when it ends. It uses this to unlock the app and to count the days since your trial started (see below).

Through App Store Connect, Apple gives us aggregated figures such as downloads, sales, refunds and renewals. If you have chosen in the iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple also gives us anonymous app usage statistics.

Anonymous usage events

To know whether Last Tin is useful, for example whether people who started the free trial still use it a week later, the app sends a few anonymous events to TelemetryDeck. We only look at aggregated numbers.

Each kind of use is sent at most once per calendar day, however many pouches you log. So the events do not reveal how much you use. Undoing an entry sends nothing. A “Had one after all” after a craving counts as a craving. The events never contain amounts, limits, savings, prices, your reasons or any other text.

Events first wait in a small outbox on your iPhone until they have been handed to TelemetryDeck. This way none is lost when you are offline, though one may occasionally arrive twice.

What TelemetryDeck adds

TelemetryDeck’s software in the app adds the following to each event:

  • An anonymous identifier. It is derived from the “identifier for vendor” that iOS assigns to the apps of one developer on one device. The app turns it into a SHA-256 hash before sending, and TelemetryDeck hashes it again on its servers. It cannot be traced back to you or your Apple Account, and it changes when you delete the app. The app does not use the advertising identifier and does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • Technical details: app version and build, device model, operating system and its version, app language, preferred language, region and locale, time zone, screen size, orientation, light or dark mode, text direction, accessibility settings (such as larger text, bold text or reduced motion), calendar details of the time the event was created (such as weekday and hour), whether the app runs from the App Store, from TestFlight or as a development build, the version of TelemetryDeck’s software, a random session number and whether the event is a test.

TelemetryDeck’s automatic session statistics are switched off in Last Tin. When sending, your iPhone necessarily connects to TelemetryDeck’s servers with its IP address; according to TelemetryDeck, IP addresses are not stored.

TelemetryDeck is operated by TelemetryDeck GmbH in Augsburg, Germany, and processes the events on servers in the European Union. More in TelemetryDeck’s privacy policy.

Email

If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to answer you and delete them when they are no longer needed. The mailbox is hosted by Google (Gmail).

Legal basis

Where the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, we process the anonymous usage events on the basis of our legitimate interest in understanding whether and how the app is used, so that we can improve it (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Emails are processed to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). The data you log in the app is not processed by us at all, because it never leaves your iPhone.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object to processing, and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. Because the usage events are anonymous, we usually cannot tell which events are yours. To delete everything you logged, delete the app. For all other requests, write to lasttin.app@gmail.com.

Age

Last Tin is intended for adults aged 18 and over.

Changes

If the app starts handling data differently, we will update this page and its date.
